V1.6PLANNEDMy Favourites · Competitor Profiles
  • Dedicated profiles for saved drivers, constructors, darts players and fighters.
  • Verified current rankings, points, divisions, teams and available career information.
  • Recent results, upcoming appearances and achievement summaries from published feeds.
  • Side-by-side comparison of up to three favourites.
  • Source links and clear unavailable-data states; no invented records or results.
V1.5PLANNEDStats & Comparisons
  • Deeper career records, historical form, head-to-heads and team comparisons.
  • Expanded fighter record analysis, darts form and tournament performance, and F1 driver/constructor comparisons.
  • Optional fight and tournament prediction tools, clearly labelled as estimates rather than results.
V1.4PLANNEDPersonal Alerts
  • Event reminders and countdown notifications.
  • Alerts for followed competitors, fight-card additions or withdrawals, results and championship changes.
  • Per-sport and per-competitor notification preferences.
V1.3IN DEVELOPMENTLive Data & Event Intelligence
  • Improve live standings, rankings and results across all five sports.
  • Add event countdowns, championship trackers, My Sports Weekend enhancements and a full results archive.
  • Add dedicated F1 race-weekend and darts tournament views, including brackets and match progress where reliable feeds permit.
  • Expand event details with confirmed start times, venues, sessions and broadcaster availability.
  • Correct PDC desktop ranking columns, strengthen duplicate-event removal across sport pages, and restore image-library diagnostics in Version History.
V1.2LIVEMy Sports Hub
  • Replace the redundant Events navigation with My Sports Weekend: a seven-day cross-sport schedule in UK local time.
  • Rename the My Weekend area to Favourites, making it the home for personally saved drivers, teams, players and fighters.
  • Restore the main televised PDC calendar and populate the PDC Order of Merit.
  • Build a curated, locally hosted image library with verified reuse rights, attribution and automatic fallbacks.
  • Introduce favourites, competitor profiles, a personal watchlist and a personalised homepage.
  • Add a central Where to Watch guide with UK broadcaster links and event-specific rights clearly distinguished.
V1.1LIVELive Sports Centre
  • Live Now overview across Formula 1, PDC Darts, Boxing, UFC and BKFC, using confirmed live status from available feeds.
  • Combined upcoming-event list for the next seven days, ordered by UK local date and time.
  • Quick route into the full five-sport calendar.
  • Version history page and discreet footer access.
  • Preserves the existing dark charcoal and gold visual theme.
V1.0FOUNDATIONFive-sport Sports Calendar
  • Formula 1, PDC Darts, Boxing, UFC and BKFC brought together in one installable PWA.
  • Dedicated sport pages, sport selector and individual event details.
  • Combined monthly calendar and event listings with sport filters.
  • Championship standings, rankings and title-holder sections where data is available.
  • UK-local event dates and times, plus event-specific viewing information where supplied.
  • Automatic scheduled data publishing, manual refresh checks and offline app-shell caching.
